✦ Our Story

Started from a WhatsApp group.

It started the way most good things do — a group chat. Five friends constantly sharing links, screenshots & recommendations for the best products they'd found online. Clothes that actually fit. Skincare that actually works. Books that genuinely changed how we think. Jewellery that looks way more expensive than it is.

At some point, we thought — why keep this to ourselves? So we built VibeKart. A place where we share everything we'd recommend to a close friend, with zero fluff and zero paid promotions.

✦ Our Mission

Honest picks. No fluff. Ever.

The internet is full of fake reviews, paid promotions & overpriced products that don't deliver. We're tired of it — and we think you are too. VibeKart exists to cut through all of that noise. Every product we feature is something we've researched, vetted & genuinely believe in. We earn a small commission when you buy through our links, but that never influences what we recommend. Our reputation matters more than any commission.
