How We Earn.

Last updated: April 2025. We believe in full transparency — here's exactly how VibeKart makes money.

1. What is Affiliate Marketing?

Affiliate marketing is a way for websites like VibeKart to earn a small commission by recommending products from other platforms. When you click a link on our website and make a purchase on Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Nykaa or any other platform we link to — we may earn a small percentage of that sale.

This comes at absolutely no extra cost to you. The price you pay is exactly the same whether you use our link or go directly to the platform.

2. Programmes We Participate In

VibeKart currently participates in the following affiliate programmes:

  • Amazon Associates — Amazon India's official affiliate programme
  • Flipkart Affiliate Programme — Flipkart's official affiliate programme
  • Myntra Affiliate Programme — for fashion & lifestyle products
  • Nykaa Affiliate Programme — for beauty & skincare products

We may add more programmes in the future and will update this page accordingly.

3. Our Picks Are Always Independent

The commission we earn never influences what we recommend. Every product featured on VibeKart is chosen because we genuinely believe it is a great pick — based on research, community reviews, ratings & trending data.

We do not accept payments from brands or sellers to feature their products. We do not do sponsored posts or paid placements. If that ever changes, we will clearly disclose it.

4. How Affiliate Links Work

When you click a "Buy ↗" button on VibeKart, you are redirected to the product page on Amazon, Flipkart or another platform. A small tracking code in the URL lets that platform know you came from VibeKart. If you complete a purchase within a certain time window (usually 24 hours for Amazon), we receive a commission.

You are never obligated to purchase anything. Clicking our links does not cost you anything.

5. Price & Availability

Product prices and availability can change at any time. The prices shown on VibeKart are approximate and may not always reflect the current price on the platform. Always check the final price on the product page before purchasing.

6. No Guarantees

While we do our best to recommend quality products, we cannot guarantee the performance, quality or satisfaction of any product we feature. All purchases are subject to the return & refund policies of the respective platforms (Amazon, Flipkart, etc.).

7. FTC & ASA Compliance

In compliance with the FTC's guidelines and advertising standards, VibeKart clearly discloses its affiliate relationships. Any page or post containing affiliate links includes a disclosure statement so you always know when we may earn a commission.

8. Questions?

If you have any questions about our affiliate relationships or how we choose our products, we'd love to hear from you. Reach out at hello@vibekart.in or visit our Contact page.
