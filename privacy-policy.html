PRIVACY NOTE

What happens when
you visit the site?

This is an informational description of the current demo build, not a substitute for a business-specific privacy policy or legal advice.

1. About this website

VibeKart is presented here as a static website-design demonstration. This version does not offer visitor accounts, checkout, payment processing, or a product-ordering database. The pages do not intentionally ask visitors to submit personal details to the website.

2. Contact links

The contact page includes an email link. Selecting it opens the visitor’s email application. The message is handled by that email provider and is not submitted to or stored by this website. The sample email address should be replaced before a real business launch.

3. Third-party services

The current design loads typography from Google Fonts and some illustrative images from Unsplash. When a browser requests these assets, the relevant service may receive technical connection information such as an IP address and browser details under its own policies.

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Hosting providers may process technical request information as part of delivering a public website. Their own privacy terms apply.

4. External links

Any external website opened from this demo is governed by that website’s own privacy practices and terms. Review those policies before sharing personal information.

5. Changes for a real launch

Before this design is adapted for a business, the privacy notice should be reviewed against the actual hosting setup, forms, analytics, advertising tools, cookies and data-processing services that the business chooses to use.

6. Questions

For questions about this demo, use the contact page. The contact address and business details should be confirmed before the website is used commercially.
