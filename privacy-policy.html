🔒 Privacy Policy

Your Privacy
Matters to Us.

Last updated: April 2025. We keep this simple & transparent — no legal jargon, just plain English.

1. Who We Are

VibeKart is a product discovery & affiliate marketing website run by a team of 5 individuals based in India. We curate trending products across Clothing, Beauty, Jewellery & Books and share affiliate links to platforms like Amazon & Flipkart.

2. What Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to improve your experience on our website. This may include:

  • Information you voluntarily provide via our Contact form (name, email, message)
  • Anonymous browsing data collected via Google Analytics (pages visited, time on site, device type)
  • Cookie data from third-party affiliate platforms (Amazon, Flipkart) when you click our links

We do not collect payment information, passwords, or any sensitive personal data.

3. How We Use Your Information

Any information we collect is used solely to:

  • Respond to your messages & queries
  • Understand how visitors use our website so we can improve it
  • Track affiliate link performance (via Amazon & Flipkart dashboards)

We never sell, trade, or share your personal information with any third party for marketing purposes.

4. Cookies

VibeKart uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience. These include:

  • Analytics cookies — via Google Analytics to understand site traffic
  • Affiliate cookies — when you click our Amazon or Flipkart links, those platforms may place cookies on your device to track purchases for commission purposes

You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time. This will not affect your ability to browse our website.

5. Third-Party Links

Our website contains links to third-party platforms such as Amazon India and Flipkart. Once you leave VibeKart and visit these platforms, their own privacy policies apply. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites.

6. Affiliate Disclosure

VibeKart participates in affiliate programmes including Amazon Associates and Flipkart Affiliate. When you click our links and make a purchase, we earn a small commission at no additional cost to you. This helps us keep the website running and free for everyone.

7. Children's Privacy

VibeKart is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, please contact us and we will delete it immediately.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to any personal data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of any communications from us

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at hello@vibekart.in

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date at the top. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us at hello@vibekart.in or visit our Contact page.
